01 / FORMAT

Fewer slides. Better questions.

The point is not to perform finished work. It is to find the person who sees your problem from a different geometry.

A

Research-first

Open problems, proof ideas, negative results, and useful counterexamples are all welcome.

B

Small by design

A capped group keeps introductions short and makes room for actual technical conversation.

C

No pitch decks

This is a peer gathering—not a recruiting session, product demo, or sponsored reception.

02 / FLOW

Coffee first. Dinner follows.

18:00

Arrive & order

Grab a drink, pick a table, and add one research keyword to the shared board.

18:15

90-second introductions

Name, affiliation, current question, and one method you want to discuss.

18:35

Three coffee-chat rounds

Three 20-minute conversations, lightly matched by topic and deliberately mixed by career stage.

19:40

Walk to dinner

A short reset—and a chance to continue the conversation that should not have ended.

20:00

Dinner & open tables

Long-form discussion, collaboration follow-ups, and local recommendations. Formal program ends at 21:30.

BRING ONE QUESTION

Conversation prompts

  • Which assumption in your current theorem feels least necessary?
  • What failure mode taught you more than a positive result?
  • Which optimization problem is ML currently formulating incorrectly?
  • What tool from another field should optimization researchers borrow next?
good conversation = specific question + unfinished thought
03 / PEOPLE

Who this is for

Researchers, PhD students, postdocs, and practitioners who engage seriously with mathematical optimization in machine learning, statistics, control, operations research, or adjacent fields.

You do not need an accepted ICML paper. You should be comfortable discussing technical research in English.

Convex optimization Nonconvex geometry Stochastic approximation Variational inequalities Optimal transport Bilevel & meta-learning Distributed optimization Optimization theory for ML Control & decision Nonsmooth methods
